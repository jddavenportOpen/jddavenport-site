If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is stored so the newsletter can be sent to you. You can unsubscribe from any issue. The site does not sell or share your information.

The Google API app

JD runs a private assistant that connects to his own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) through Google's OAuth. It is not offered to the public and is used only with accounts JD owns.

  • It reads and acts on data in JD's own account only, to triage his email, manage his calendar and work with his documents.
  • Access tokens are stored on JD's own computer. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
